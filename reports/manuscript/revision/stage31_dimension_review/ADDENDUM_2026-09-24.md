# Addendum — the review task has changed

**Issued 2026-09-24. Read this before the bundle's own README.**

The seven files in this directory are unchanged and remain correct as computation. **Nothing in
them has been recomputed, corrected or withdrawn.** What has changed is the question they were
assembled to answer.

## What the bundle's README asks, and why that is no longer the question

The frozen bundle asks a reviewer to **select a fixed `n_prin_comps`** for Stage 31 Scrublet, then
audit the configuration and separately authorise a v2 detection run.

**Do not answer that question.** A ruling recorded on 2026-09-23 (`docs/decisions.md`,
`docs/execution-plan.md`, commit `acf969273e36d18c1fcc1f311c9e9d2f1754e15b`) stopped dimension
selection for the current design.

The reason is the order of operations, not the arithmetic. Stage 31 currently caps the analytic
data at **60 cells per patient** and only then runs Scrublet independently by `Sample_ID`, so an
eligible group holds **30–60 cells**. The dimension audit in this bundle establishes that a PC
count is *computationally feasible* on such a group. It does not establish that the resulting
neighbourhood model is *scientifically valid*. Feasibility at a chosen PC count is not validity.

## What this bundle is now

**A historical feasibility attachment.** It is evidence about the old subsample-first design:

- 301 unique `Sample_ID`s measured, 161 satisfying the existing cell-count rule, 0 recorded
  measurement errors;
- 59 integer candidates and 8,247 exclusion records, recomputed independently and byte-identical;
- **no PCA dimension was selected** — the audit was measurement-only.

Those numbers were independently re-verified on 2026-09-24 and passed. They describe a design that
is being replaced, which is precisely why they must not be read as a pending parameter choice.

## What the reviewer is asked to rule on instead

The current question is the **workflow redesign**, ahead of any parameter:

> Should Scrublet run on the complete, **pre-subsample** per-capture/sample raw-count input, with
> calls and status mapped onto the 60-cell analytic subset afterward?

Please rule on each of the following, with an explicit "insufficient evidence, do not proceed"
available on every one:

1. **Analysis unit** — is the per-capture/sample raw-count matrix the right unit for detection, and
   what makes a unit admissible?
2. **Raw-count source** — which input counts as the complete pre-subsample matrix, and what must be
   true of it before it is used?
3. **Capture mapping** — under what conditions may a biological sample be attributed to a physical
   capture? Treat the equivalence of the **analysis channel** and the **physical capture** as
   something that must be independently established per cohort, not assumed. No capture-provenance
   draft is supplied as reviewed evidence in this package, and none should be relied on.
4. **Score-to-subset mapping** — on what basis may a call or status computed on the full input be
   carried onto the 60-cell subset, and what would invalidate that transfer?
5. **Cross-sample comparability** — what must hold for scores from different captures to be
   compared at all?
6. **Failure conditions** — what observation retrospectively invalidates a run performed this way?

## Sequencing — the parameter question comes last

Selecting a fixed `n_prin_comps` is **deferred**, and deliberately so. The order is:

1. the workflow redesign above is approved;
2. a **new** dimension audit is run against the new population — the complete pre-subsample inputs,
   whose cell counts per unit are not the 30–60 of this bundle, so this bundle's 161/301 figures do
   not carry over;
3. only then is a dimension selected.

Choosing a value now would be choosing it for a population that will not be scored.

## Standing constraints

- If the complete inputs or the mappings cannot be recovered, the Stage 31 doublet-rate outcome
  analysis **leaves the evidence chain** rather than proceeding on the old design.
- No Stage 31 detection or outcome job is authorised. No runtime configuration exists.
- A mechanical doublet candidate is not a deletion authorisation, and no final-authorisation
  artifact exists.
