    Results¶

    Provisional historical results. This page predates the 2026-09 eight-cohort QC rebuild. Do not use these values as current evidence; see ../current-status.md. Regenerate this page after P2/P3 completes.

    Headline numbers from the BIB submission (manuscript ~97% complete, May 2026). All AUCs use patient-level StratifiedGroupKFold with Sample_ID (413 unique donors in the COVID cohort).

    Cross-disease bidirectional transfer¶

    Train COVID-mortality → test sepsis-mortality, and reverse. Same LR hyperplane applied across diseases — a pure test of embedding-space geometry.

    Model COVID → Sepsis Sepsis → COVID Pattern
    STATE 0.800 0.706 Bidirectional generalizer
    STACK 0.917 0.698 Bidirectional generalizer
    UCE 0.798 0.514 Forward-only
    scPRINT-2 0.855 0.543 Forward-only
    TF 0.775 0.629 Moderate bidirectional
    Geneformer 0.742 0.581 Weak bidirectional
    scGPT 0.352 ⚠ 0.555 Anti-correlated (CI [0.22, 0.50])

    scGPT systematically inverts mortality prediction sign across diseases, likely because its 512-dim embedding compresses platelet activation — hyper-activated in COVID-fatal but exhausted in sepsis-fatal.

    Adaptation curve (the null finding)¶

    PCA-200+LR from-scratch vs FMs, varying labelled sepsis cells n added to the COVID training pool. 10 seeds per n, patient-stratified holdout.

    Method n=0 (zero-shot) n=100 Asymptote
    PCA-200+LR 0.891 0.936 0.964
    scPRINT-2 0.920 0.931 0.950
    STATE 0.882 0.912 0.948
    TranscriptFormer 0.811 0.860 0.912
    Geneformer 0.804 0.833 0.878
    UCE 0.646 0.756 0.876
    STACK 0.643 0.887 0.890

    No FM has both higher Y-intercept AND steeper early slope than the from-scratch baseline. Only scPRINT-2 beats PCA on the zero-shot intercept, but it is already flat (slope +0.010 to n=100). PCA's asymptote beats every FM at full data.

    Multimodal fusion (the silver lining)¶

    Late-averaging predicted probabilities across all 6 FMs beats any single FM (AUC 0.849 vs STATE's 0.831). Suggests the FMs encode partially non-redundant information — supported by the SHAP-Jaccard mechanism diagnostic showing two distinct biological axes.

    What this means for the field¶

    1. Patient-level CV must be the floor for any clinical scFM benchmark. Cell-level CV reports AUCs that are 7–17 percentage points inflated.
    2. PCA + LR is a required baseline. Any FM that does not beat PCA on the target task does not justify its computational cost.
    3. High AUC is necessary but not sufficient. Two FMs at the same AUC can encode incompatible biology — verify with the Diagnostic Suite before deploying.
    4. Multimodal fusion is currently the best deployment strategy. No single FM dominates across robustness, calibration, and generalisation.
