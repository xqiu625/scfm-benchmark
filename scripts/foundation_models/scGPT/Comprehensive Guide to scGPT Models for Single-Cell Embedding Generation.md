# Comprehensive Guide to scGPT Models for Single-Cell Embedding Generation

## Introduction reveals transformer potential for genomic analysis

scGPT represents the pioneering application of generative pre-trained transformers to single-cell genomics, marking a fundamental shift in how we approach cellular data analysis. First introduced in 2023 by researchers at the University of Toronto and published in Nature Methods 2024, scGPT adapts the conceptual framework of language models to biological data by treating cells as "sentences" and genes as "words." The model, pre-trained on over **33 million human single-cell RNA-sequencing profiles**, demonstrates that transformer architectures can effectively capture the complex relationships within cellular expression patterns.

The foundation model paradigm offers a compelling vision for single-cell analysis: a single pre-trained model that can be fine-tuned for diverse downstream tasks including cell type annotation, batch integration, multi-omics analysis, and perturbation prediction. This approach promises to reduce the need for task-specific models and enable knowledge transfer across datasets and experimental conditions. However, recent critical evaluations in 2024-2025 have revealed significant limitations in zero-shot performance, raising important questions about current training paradigms and the practical utility of these models compared to simpler baseline methods.

The significance of scGPT extends beyond technical innovation to represent a broader trend toward foundation models in computational biology. As the field rapidly evolves with new variants like scGPT-spatial for spatial transcriptomics and massive scaling efforts reaching 800 million parameters with models like CellFM, understanding both the capabilities and limitations of these approaches becomes essential for researchers navigating this transformative technology.

## Technical architecture employs specialized transformer design

The scGPT architecture builds upon a **12-layer decoder-only transformer** with 8 attention heads and 512-dimensional embeddings, totaling approximately 100 million parameters. This design specifically addresses the unique challenges of single-cell data through several key innovations. The model employs a three-layer input embedding strategy combining gene tokens (unique identifiers for each gene), expression values (discretized into 51 bins), and condition tokens (encoding metadata like batch, modality, or perturbation states). This multi-faceted representation enables the model to simultaneously capture gene identity, expression levels, and experimental context within a unified framework.

A critical architectural innovation involves the adaptation of attention mechanisms for non-sequential biological data. Unlike text where word order matters, gene expression lacks inherent sequential structure. scGPT addresses this through learned positional embeddings that capture gene interaction patterns rather than sequential positions. The model introduces specialized attention masking strategies for generative pre-training, enabling joint optimization of cell and gene representations through dual prediction pathways. The architecture first predicts masked gene expressions from unmasked genes, then refines predictions using learned cell embeddings, creating a bidirectional flow of information between gene-level and cell-level representations.

The training methodology centers on masked gene prediction, analogous to masked language modeling in NLP. During pre-training, the model randomly masks **40% of genes** and learns to predict both gene identity and expression levels. This self-supervised approach enables learning from unlabeled data at scale. The model employs expression value binning to handle the continuous nature of gene expression while maintaining computational efficiency. Training on the massive CELLxGENE dataset requires sophisticated distributed computing strategies, with the full pre-training process taking weeks on multiple GPUs. Fine-tuning for specific tasks typically requires 10-30 epochs with smaller batch sizes and task-specific objectives like adversarial training for batch correction or contrastive learning for cell embedding refinement.

## Comparison with alternatives reveals performance trade-offs

When evaluated against other single-cell embedding methods, scGPT presents a complex picture of strengths and limitations. Compared to scBERT, another transformer-based approach, scGPT operates **2x faster** but requires **30x more memory**, highlighting a significant efficiency trade-off. While scGPT uses GPT-style autoregressive generation with more parameters, scBERT's bidirectional encoding with fewer parameters shows more stable performance on imbalanced datasets and better handling of rare cell types. Against Geneformer, which uses rank-ordered gene lists rather than binned expression values, scGPT shows less severe overfitting but both models surprisingly underperform simple baselines in zero-shot scenarios.

The comparison with variational autoencoder approaches like scVI reveals fundamental differences in modeling philosophy. scVI's probabilistic framework consistently outperforms scGPT in zero-shot settings and batch integration tasks, suggesting that explicit modeling of technical and biological variation may be more effective than transformer-based pattern learning for certain applications. Perhaps most surprisingly, traditional methods like highly variable gene (HVG) selection often match or exceed scGPT's performance in zero-shot scenarios while requiring minimal computational resources. Harmony, a specialized batch correction method, consistently outperforms scGPT in removing technical artifacts while preserving biological signals.

Recent benchmarking studies from 2024-2025 have revealed that for perturbation prediction tasks, simple baseline models including mean prediction and random forests with Gene Ontology features achieve Pearson correlations of **0.48-0.74** compared to scGPT's **0.33-0.64** across multiple datasets. These findings challenge the assumption that increased model complexity necessarily translates to improved performance and highlight the importance of careful evaluation against appropriate baselines. The performance gap appears particularly pronounced in zero-shot settings, where foundation models struggle to generalize without fine-tuning, raising questions about whether current pre-training objectives effectively capture biologically meaningful patterns.

## Data preprocessing shapes model performance critically

Successful scGPT implementation requires a standardized preprocessing pipeline that significantly influences model performance. The process begins with gene filtering to remove lowly expressed genes (typically those with counts below 3 across all cells), followed by optional cell filtering based on quality metrics. Total count normalization to 10,000 reads per cell addresses sequencing depth variation, followed by log1p transformation to stabilize variance. The selection of **1,200-3,000 highly variable genes** using methods like Seurat_v3 focuses the model on biologically informative features while managing computational requirements.

The critical step of expression value binning discretizes continuous expression levels into 51 bins, creating relative representations that address scale differences across sequencing protocols. This binning strategy, while enabling efficient transformer processing, introduces quantization artifacts that may impact biological signal preservation. The preprocessing pipeline must also handle batch effects through condition tokens that encode technical metadata, enabling the model to learn batch-invariant representations during training. Quality control steps include removal of doublets, filtering of mitochondrial and ribosomal genes (when appropriate), and assessment of batch mixing to ensure technical artifacts don't dominate biological variation.

Input data must be formatted as AnnData objects with specific structure requirements: the expression matrix in the X field, gene metadata in var, and cell metadata in obs. The framework supports sparse matrix representations for efficient handling of the high sparsity typical in single-cell data (often >90% zeros). Careful attention to preprocessing parameters significantly impacts downstream performance - for instance, the choice of HVG selection method and the number of selected genes can alter cell type classification accuracy by 5-10%. Recent studies suggest that preprocessing choices may explain some of the performance variation observed across different scGPT implementations and highlight the need for standardized preprocessing protocols.

## Applications demonstrate versatility across analysis tasks

scGPT's primary applications span the major challenges in single-cell analysis, with varying degrees of success across different tasks. For **cell type classification**, fine-tuned models achieve accuracy exceeding 90% on well-curated datasets like PBMC, demonstrating competitive performance with specialized classifiers. The model successfully handles multi-batch integration across datasets with 100,000+ cells, though it typically underperforms dedicated batch correction methods like Harmony and scVI in preserving biological variation while removing technical artifacts.

Multi-omics integration represents a promising application where scGPT successfully combines RNA-seq with ATAC-seq and protein data from CITE-seq experiments. The model's ability to handle diverse data modalities through condition tokens enables cross-modal prediction and unified cellular representations across molecular layers. In trajectory inference and developmental biology applications, scGPT embeddings capture differentiation trajectories and can identify intermediate cell states, though the interpretability of these trajectories remains challenging compared to methods explicitly designed for pseudotime analysis.

For **perturbation prediction** in drug discovery and genetic screening contexts, results have been disappointing. Recent benchmarking on Perturb-seq datasets shows scGPT achieving Pearson correlations of 0.33-0.64, consistently underperforming simple mean-based predictors (0.71) and random forest models with biological features (0.48-0.74). The model shows particular weakness in predicting responses to unseen perturbations, limiting its utility for discovering novel therapeutic targets. More successful applications include gene regulatory network inference through attention weight analysis, where the model captures some biologically meaningful gene-gene relationships, though with weaker associations to known pathways compared to methods incorporating prior biological knowledge.

## Implementation landscape offers multiple access points

The scGPT ecosystem provides various implementation options catering to different technical expertise levels. The **official GitHub repository** (github.com/bowang-lab/scGPT) with 2,400+ stars serves as the primary resource, offering comprehensive code, pretrained models, and tutorial notebooks. Installation via pip (`pip install scgpt "flash-attn<1.0.5"`) integrates with the scanpy ecosystem, though users should note compatibility constraints including the requirement for PyTorch 1.13 rather than newer versions. Docker containers like `xueerchen/scgpt:0.1.7` provide the easiest setup path, avoiding complex dependency management while ensuring GPU support and consistent environments across systems.

For researchers preferring graphical interfaces, the **Superbio.ai platform** offers no-code web applications for cell annotation, reference mapping, and gene regulatory network inference with cloud GPU support. The Virtual Cells Platform provides interactive tutorials and quickstart guides accessible through a browser. These platforms democratize access to scGPT capabilities without requiring programming expertise, though they may lack the flexibility needed for custom analyses. Integration with Hugging Face (huggingface.co/tdc/scGPT) enables standardized model loading and sharing, while specialized extensions like scGPT-spatial expand capabilities to spatial transcriptomics applications.

Available pretrained models include the whole-human model trained on 33 million cells (recommended for most applications), tissue-specific models for brain (13.2M cells), blood and bone marrow (10.3M cells), and other organs. Hardware requirements vary by application but typically include an NVIDIA GPU with CUDA 11.7+ support and sufficient memory for model parameters and data batches. The framework supports distributed training across multiple GPUs for large-scale applications, with inference speed reaching 0.1 seconds for 4,000 cells on GPU compared to 7 seconds on CPU.

## Performance benchmarks reveal critical limitations

Comprehensive evaluation of scGPT across standard benchmarks reveals a nuanced performance profile with significant variation across tasks and evaluation modes. In cell type classification with fine-tuning, the model achieves **F1 scores exceeding 0.8** on well-curated datasets, with 96.7% accuracy reported for pancreas datasets. However, zero-shot performance tells a different story: average silhouette width scores of 0.53 and adjusted rand indices often below 0.6 indicate substantial room for improvement in generalization without task-specific training.

Batch integration benchmarks show mixed results, with scGPT achieving reasonable batch mixing scores but consistently underperforming specialized methods. Batch average silhouette width scores typically fall below those of Harmony and scVI, while integration of local inverse Simpson's index (iLISI) and cell-type local inverse Simpson's index (cLISI) metrics reveal challenges in simultaneously preserving biological variation and removing technical artifacts. The model's computational requirements further complicate deployment: memory usage approximately **30 times higher** than scBERT and extensive pre-training requiring weeks on multiple GPUs limit accessibility for many research groups.

Most concerning are results from perturbation prediction benchmarks, where scGPT achieves Pearson correlations of 0.641, 0.554, and 0.327-0.596 on the Adamson, Norman, and Replogle datasets respectively, consistently underperforming mean baseline predictions and basic machine learning models. These results suggest fundamental limitations in the model's ability to capture causal biological relationships and raise questions about the suitability of current pre-training objectives for predictive tasks requiring understanding of biological mechanisms rather than pattern recognition.

## Current limitations demand careful consideration

The most significant limitation of scGPT and similar foundation models lies in their **poor zero-shot performance**, consistently underperforming simple baselines without fine-tuning. This finding, confirmed across multiple independent evaluations in 2024-2025, challenges the fundamental premise of foundation models as general-purpose tools and suggests that current masked language modeling approaches may not effectively capture transferable biological knowledge. The models struggle particularly with batch effect handling, often performing worse than traditional methods in integrating datasets with strong technical variation.

Technical limitations include extreme computational requirements that place these models beyond reach for many research groups. The tokenization process through expression binning introduces artifacts that may obscure subtle biological signals, while the lack of explicit biological constraints means models can learn spurious correlations that don't reflect true biological relationships. Current evaluation benchmarks present additional challenges: many datasets used for evaluation overlap with training data, confounding performance assessments, while the low biological variance in existing perturbation prediction benchmarks may favor simple statistical approaches over complex models.

Interpretability remains a major challenge despite attention weight visualization capabilities. The biological meaning of learned representations often remains opaque, making it difficult to validate novel predictions or understand model failures. The gap between computational predictions and experimental validation grows wider as models become more complex, while the lack of uncertainty quantification limits the reliability of model outputs for hypothesis generation. These limitations suggest that current foundation models may be better suited for specific applications where large-scale pattern recognition adds value rather than as universal solutions for single-cell analysis.

## Recent advances push boundaries while exposing challenges

The 2024-2025 period has witnessed rapid evolution in single-cell foundation models, with **scGPT-spatial** representing a major advance in spatial transcriptomics integration. This extension employs continual pretraining on 30 million spatial profiles, introducing a mixture of experts decoder for protocol-aware processing and neighborhood-based training objectives that capture spatial co-localization patterns. The model demonstrates superior performance in multi-slide integration and contextualized gene expression imputation, opening new possibilities for understanding tissue organization and cell-cell communication.

Scaling efforts have reached unprecedented levels with **CellFM achieving 800 million parameters** trained on 100 million human cells - an 8-fold increase over previous models. Built on a modified RetNet framework for improved efficiency, CellFM shows 1.6-1.94% improvement in gene function prediction tasks. The scFoundation model (100M parameters) introduces asymmetric transformer architectures and demonstrates state-of-the-art performance in drug response prediction and perturbation modeling when properly fine-tuned. Alternative approaches like GenePT, which leverages ChatGPT embeddings of gene descriptions, achieve comparable performance without extensive dataset curation, suggesting creative integration of existing language models may offer efficient alternatives to training specialized biological models from scratch.

However, critical evaluation studies have tempered enthusiasm by revealing fundamental challenges. Comprehensive zero-shot benchmarking shows foundation models significantly underperforming simple baselines, with highly variable gene selection often achieving better results than complex transformer models. Analysis of perturbation prediction reveals that even mean prediction baselines outperform foundation models, while random forests using Gene Ontology features achieve substantially better correlations. These findings have prompted serious reconsideration of current training paradigms and evaluation practices, suggesting that the field may need to fundamentally rethink approaches to building foundation models for biology.

## Practical guidance emphasizes pragmatic implementation

Researchers implementing scGPT should begin with realistic expectations and careful consideration of whether the model's capabilities justify its computational costs for their specific application. For initial exploration, the **Docker container approach** (`docker pull xueerchen/scgpt:0.1.7`) offers the most straightforward setup, avoiding dependency conflicts while providing GPU support. Those requiring programmatic access should use the pip installation with careful attention to version constraints, particularly the PyTorch 1.13 requirement and flash-attention compatibility.

Data preparation critically influences outcomes - standardize preprocessing using the provided pipeline with 1,200-3,000 highly variable genes, ensuring consistent normalization (10,000 counts per cell) and appropriate binning (51 bins default). For cell type annotation tasks where labeled reference data exists, fine-tuning typically yields good results with 15-30 epochs at learning rate 1e-4 and batch size 64. Start with the whole-human pretrained model for general applications, switching to tissue-specific models only when working with specialized datasets. Monitor GPU memory usage carefully and reduce batch sizes if encountering out-of-memory errors.

Consider simpler alternatives before committing to scGPT: for batch integration, Harmony or scVI often provide better results with less computational overhead; for cell type annotation, logistic regression on highly variable genes may suffice; for perturbation prediction, random forest models with biological features consistently outperform transformer approaches. When scGPT is appropriate - particularly for multi-omics integration or when leveraging pretrained representations for transfer learning - focus on careful fine-tuning rather than expecting strong zero-shot performance. Document preprocessing choices meticulously as these significantly impact reproducibility, and validate critical findings with orthogonal methods given current limitations in model interpretability.

## Future directions target biological understanding

The future of single-cell foundation models centers on the ambitious vision of **AI Virtual Cells (AIVCs)** - comprehensive computational models that simulate molecular, cellular, and tissue-level processes with high fidelity. These systems would integrate diverse data types including genomics, proteomics, metabolomics, and imaging to create predictive models of cellular behavior. Development priorities include incorporating causal modeling capabilities, enabling counterfactual reasoning for hypothesis testing, and creating interpretable outputs that guide experimental design. Success requires moving beyond pattern recognition to encode biological mechanisms and constraints explicitly.

Methodological improvements must address current limitations through enhanced pre-training objectives that better capture biological relationships. Proposed approaches include incorporating gene regulatory networks and protein-protein interactions as inductive biases, developing evaluation frameworks with reserved benchmark datasets never used in training, and creating metrics that assess biological relevance rather than statistical performance alone. The integration of spatial and temporal dynamics through 4D modeling (space plus time) promises to capture developmental processes and tissue organization, while advances in multimodal learning could unify morphological, molecular, and functional cellular characteristics.

Clinical translation represents both opportunity and challenge, with precision medicine applications requiring models that predict patient-specific treatment responses reliably. Key developments needed include federated learning approaches that preserve patient privacy while enabling large-scale training, uncertainty quantification methods that communicate prediction confidence, and regulatory frameworks for deploying AI models in clinical decision-making. The convergence of single-cell genomics with other biomedical AI efforts, including protein structure prediction and drug design, suggests integrated platforms that span from molecular to organism-level modeling may emerge. Success will require interdisciplinary collaboration between computational biologists, experimentalists, and clinicians to ensure models capture biological reality while providing actionable insights for research and medicine.

## Conclusion

scGPT represents both the promise and current limitations of foundation models in single-cell biology. While demonstrating that transformer architectures can process biological data at unprecedented scale and showing strong performance in specific fine-tuned applications, the model's poor zero-shot generalization and high computational requirements raise important questions about the cost-benefit trade-off for many research applications. The rapid evolution of the field, exemplified by spatial extensions and massive scaling efforts, indicates continued investment in this approach despite current challenges.

Researchers should approach scGPT and similar models with informed pragmatism - leveraging their capabilities for appropriate tasks while recognizing when simpler methods may be more suitable. The future success of foundation models in biology will likely depend on fundamental advances in training objectives, biological knowledge integration, and evaluation standards rather than simply scaling existing approaches. As the field matures, the focus must shift from demonstrating technical feasibility to delivering genuine biological insights and practical utility that justifies the substantial computational investment these models require.
